# Chess corpus credits

ChessTrace's curated tournament corpus uses official Lichess Broadcast exports under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. The importer selects games from those archives and may combine adjacent PGN comments or normalize quotation marks to keep replay compatible. The Lichess source and license apply to the selected game data. See the source register for attribution and archive details.
