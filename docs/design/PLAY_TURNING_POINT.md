# Play the Turning Point

A due puzzle starts at its saved pre-move FEN, oriented for the side to move. The player can tap a piece and destination or drag a piece. Chess.js checks legal moves, including castling and en passant. A promotion waits for a queen, rook, bishop, or knight choice before the move is submitted. The first hint highlights the saved solution's piece, and the second highlights its destination. Reveal shows the saved line. Revealing before playing opens the existing self-rating flow and creates no move attempt. Older puzzles without a legal saved solution also support self-rating.

The saved solution is the first legal move from its PGN, replayed from the stored FEN. Turning Points saves positive mate distance for new puzzles. A stored mating move or a positive forced-mate score means only the saved move qualifies. For other positions, Stockfish compares unrestricted and candidate-only searches at depth 12 from the same root position and side. A candidate within the configured centipawn tolerance qualifies as “different, but also strong.” The default tolerance is 50 centipawns. A mate score is handled separately from a centipawn score. When the worker cannot finish grading, the player can retry or reveal without recording an ungraded move. [Stockfish developer instructions](https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/stockfish-wiki/Developers.html)

The authenticated attempt endpoint validates ownership, legality, and grading inputs. The API derives correctness using the stored solution and the browser's evaluation, since engine work stays in the browser. A client-generated attempt ID makes retrying a submission safe. Each attempt snapshots the puzzle's motifs, so later edits leave historical rates intact. The existing review stats endpoint returns move-based and self-rated weekly totals over the same 90-day window. Move-based totals include plays with and without hints and exclude reveals without a move.

The review screen suggests Good for a correct move, Hard for a correct move with a hint, and Again for a wrong move. The user chooses the actual rating through the existing review endpoint and can override any suggestion. Attempt recording and SM-2 scheduling are separate.
